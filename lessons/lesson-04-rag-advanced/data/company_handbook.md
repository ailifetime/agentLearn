# Company Handbook

## Annual Leave Policy

Employees receive 10 days of paid annual leave during their first two years.
After completing two full years, the allowance increases to 15 days per year.
Annual leave requests should be submitted at least 5 business days in advance.
Unused annual leave can be carried over for one quarter into the next calendar year.

## Knowledge Base Workflow

The support team maintains an internal knowledge base for product FAQs, incident runbooks, and onboarding guides.
When documentation is updated, the content team republishes the Markdown source and rebuilds the retrieval index.
Each knowledge base article should focus on a single topic so retrieval chunks stay semantically consistent.

## Retention Review Notes

The growth team reviews user retention every Friday.
Weekly retention reports compare new-user retention, activation rate, and support ticket volume.
Retention experiments must be documented with a hypothesis, owner, and success metric.

## AI Platform Notes

RAG stands for retrieval-augmented generation.
RAG first retrieves relevant external knowledge and then uses that context to generate an answer.
Fine-tuning changes model behavior through additional training, while RAG keeps the base model unchanged and injects knowledge at runtime.
For multi-turn chat, query rewriting helps convert follow-up questions into standalone search queries.

## Customer Support Policy

Support agents should cite the knowledge base article or policy source when answering customer questions.
If no trustworthy source is found, the agent should say that the answer could not be verified.
Escalations about leave policy should include the employee region and contract type before a final answer is sent.
